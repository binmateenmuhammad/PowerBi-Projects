# 📊 2024 IT Jobs Demand Insights Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing global IT job trends, demand, and salary insights for 2024.

---

## 🌟 Project Overview
This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the IT job market in 2024. It is designed to help job seekers, recruiters, and HR professionals understand which technologies are in high demand, where the opportunities are concentrated, and how salaries vary across different regions.

### 🔍 Key Features
* **Global Demand Map:** Geographic visualization of IT job openings.
* **Role-Based Insights:** Breakdown of demand for Software Engineers, Data Scientists, DevOps, etc.
* **Skill-set Analysis:** Identification of top-tier technical skills required in 2024.
* **Dynamic Filtering:** Filter data by **Country**, **Job Title**, **Company**, and **Employment Type**.
* **Year-over-Year (YoY) Growth:** Comparative trends from previous years (if applicable).

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Data
* **Tool:** Power BI Desktop
* **Data Source:** https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1E0jTbLjATDW7TD8Et9e4F1KPCEfbJhb1
* **Transformation:** Power Query (M Language) used for data cleaning and normalization.

### 🏠 1. Job Trends (Home)
The primary entry point for high-level market statistics.
* **Global Demand Map:** Visualizes job density across continents.
* **Median Pay Analysis:** A comparative bar chart for major roles (Software Engineer, Data Scientist, etc.).
* **Pay Toggle:** Interactive buttons to switch between **Hourly** and **Annual** pay views.
* **Remote Status:** Quick-view cards for remote work availability.

### 🛠️ 2. Job Data (Role Deep-Dive)
Detailed analysis focused on specific career paths like **Data Engineering**.
* **Degree Requirements:** Breakdown of jobs requiring a university degree vs. those that don't.
* **Skill Treemap:** Identification of the **Top 10 Skills** (e.g., SQL, Python, AWS, Snowflake).
* **Salary Gauge:** Average salary visualization relative to market benchmarks ($130K avg).

### 🇨🇦 3. Country Analysis
Localized insights for specific regions (e.g., Canada).
* **Market Share:** Displays the country’s portion of the global job market.
* **Regional Demand:** A treemap of the most requested job titles within that specific country.
* **Salary Benchmarks:** Targeted average salary gauges for the local currency/market.

### 🏢 4. Top Job Offering Companies
Focuses on the companies driving the 2024 hiring landscape.
* **Pay Leaders:** Top companies by median salary (e.g., Goldman Tech, Netflix).
* **Posting Volume:** Companies with the highest recruitment activity (e.g., Capital One, Dice).
* **Advanced Filters:** Ability to filter company data by **Job Title** and **Degree Requirement**.
